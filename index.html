WHO IS MAX VERSTAPPEN?

Max Emilian Verstappen is a Dutch Formula One racing driver for Oracle Red Bull Racing. He is a four-time Formula One World Drivers' Champion (2021–2024). Renowned for his aggressive, uncompromising racecraft and raw pace, he shattered the sport's age barriers by becoming both the youngest driver to start a Grand Prix (17) and the youngest to win one (18). In 2023, he delivered the most dominant campaign in Formula One history, winning a record-breaking 19 out of 22 races.
